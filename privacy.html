shipE Privacy Policy & Data Practices

Last updated: September 2026

shipE is a web-based video game developed by Korvyn Games, a product of Auduvant Technologies ("we," "our," or "us").

This Privacy Policy & Data Practices explains how information is handled when you use shipE.

Our goal is to keep shipE simple and privacy-conscious. The game does not require an account, registration, or user profile.

1. About shipE

shipE is a browser-based business and investment simulation game designed around startup, entrepreneurship, and business-management concepts.

The game does not require users to:

  • Create an account
  • Register
  • Provide their name
  • Provide an email address
  • Create a user profile
  • Provide payment information
  • Provide personal information to play

2. Information We Collect

We do not intentionally collect personal information from players through shipE.

shipE does not intentionally collect:

  • Names
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Home addresses
  • User accounts or usernames
  • Passwords
  • Financial account information
  • Government identification
  • Precise location information
  • User profiles
  • Individual gameplay histories tied to a person
  • Screen recordings
  • Keystroke data
  • Advertising profiles
  • Cross-site tracking information

shipE does not intentionally collect usage analytics or maintain individual player profiles.

3. Cookies and Tracking

shipE does not intentionally use cookies or similar technologies to track individual players.

We do not intentionally use behavioral advertising, cross-site tracking, or persistent identifiers to monitor users.

4. Hosting and Third-Party Infrastructure

shipE is hosted using third-party web infrastructure, including GitHub Pages and related GitHub services.

Because shipE is hosted by a third-party provider, that provider may independently collect technical information about visitors as part of operating, securing, and maintaining its infrastructure.

Such third-party collection is controlled by the applicable provider and is separate from information intentionally collected by Korvyn Games through shipE.

Users should review the applicable third-party provider's privacy policies for information about data collected by those services.

5. Children's Privacy

shipE is a general-audience business game.

The game is designed around business, entrepreneurship, startups, and investment concepts and does not intentionally target children with child-directed content.

shipE does not require users to provide their age or date of birth.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through shipE.

If we become aware that personal information belonging to a child under 13 has been provided to us through shipE or through a related service, we may take appropriate steps to delete that information.

6. Game Content

shipE is a fictional business and investment simulation.

The game does not contain or promote:

  • Gambling
  • Alcohol
  • Tobacco or nicotine products
  • Adult sexual content
  • Illegal drugs
  • Real-money wagering

The game is intended as a simulation and entertainment product and does not constitute financial, investment, legal, tax, or business advice.

7. Data Security

Because shipE does not intentionally collect personal information from players, there is limited player information maintained by us through the game.

We nevertheless take reasonable measures to protect information under our control from unauthorized access, alteration, or misuse.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy & Data Practices when shipE's features, hosting arrangements, or legal requirements change.

The "Last updated" date at the beginning of this policy indicates when the policy was most recently revised.

9. Contact

Questions or concerns regarding shipE or this Privacy Policy may be directed to:
